# Launch draft

Use a real, consented editing session for the launch video. The bundled sample is clearly labeled as illustrative and is not proof of recording a real DaVinci session.

## English

I made TimelineLapse: a small open-source tool that turns the making of an edit into a timelapse of your actual timeline.

Choose your editor's window, crop to the tracks, and let it save screenshots while you work. At the end, replay the session and export a short MP4. Everything runs locally.

It works by recording the visible window, so it doesn't need Resolve Studio's scripting API. No project modification, account, subscription, or watermark.

It's an early alpha. I'd love feedback from editors on window sharing, crop stability, and whether filtering nearly identical screenshots helps in long sessions.

## Español

Hice TimelineLapse: una herramienta open source que convierte el proceso de edición en un timelapse de tu timeline real.

Elegís la ventana de tu editor, recortás la zona de pistas y la herramienta guarda capturas mientras trabajás. Al terminar, exportás un MP4. Todo se procesa en tu computadora.

Es una primera versión. Busco editores que quieran probar una sesión y contarme si el registro representa bien su proceso.

## First demonstration

1. Record a short, real session beginning with a nearly empty timeline.
2. Add, trim, rearrange, and remove clips; add music and a title.
3. Export the native timeline view and a 12-second landscape presentation.
4. Show the finished edit briefly, then the timeline's evolution.
5. Link the source and ask for one specific piece of feedback.

Measure successful recordings and repeat use along with GitHub stars. Validate the core capture experience before promoting widely.
